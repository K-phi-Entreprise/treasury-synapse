Add your LinkedIn connections

  1. Open Download your data on LinkedIn (Settings, Data privacy, Download your data).
  2. Choose Download larger data archive (the first option: it is the one that includes connections), then Request archive.
  3. LinkedIn emails you when it is ready: a first file often within minutes, the full archive within a day. Download the zip and drop it below as it is.

Only people already on this map are matched, by name and company. Nobody new is added, and emails in the file are ignored. Matches become connections in the Connections view once they have been reviewed; they never change anyone's size on the map.
