Add your LinkedIn connections

  1. On LinkedIn: Settings, Data privacy, Get a copy of your data, Connections.
  2. LinkedIn emails you a file called Connections.csv, usually within minutes.
  3. Drop it below. It is read here, in your browser, and never uploaded.

Only people already on this map are matched, by name and company. Nobody new is added, and emails in the file are ignored. Matches become connections in the Connections view once they have been reviewed; they never change anyone's size on the map.
